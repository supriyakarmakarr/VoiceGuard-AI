    04 / THE PREVENTION CENTER

    A familiar voice.
    A reason to verify.

    Prevent voice-cloning impersonation attacks.
    Build a second layer of trust.

    ◇Verify sensitive requests↗

    A voice alone is not proof of identity. Pause before approving payments, account changes or requests for credentials.

    ⇄Use a second channel↗

    Call a saved, independently verified number or use your organization’s trusted messaging channel. Do not rely on contact details supplied by the caller.

    ?Make verification unpredictable↗

    Use a fresh challenge or agreed verification process. A challenge is an additional check; a convincing answer is not sufficient on its own.

    ◌Limit public voice exposure↗

    Consider where you publish long, clean voice recordings. Share recordings with the audience that actually needs them.

    ⌁Notice unusual behavior↗

    Urgency, secrecy, unfamiliar payment details and attempts to bypass normal procedures are reasons to pause and verify.

    ◎Add an evidence layer↗

    Use VoiceGuard to inspect acoustic signals. A low concern score does not establish authenticity; keep independent verification in place.
